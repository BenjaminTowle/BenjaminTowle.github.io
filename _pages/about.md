I am a third year CS PhD student at the University of Nottingham, studying under [Ke (Adam) Zhou](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/computerscience/people/ke.zhou) and [Xin Chen](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/computerscience/people/xin.chen), having previously completed an undergraduate degree at the University of Oxford and a master's degree also at University of Nottingham. My research focuses on the application of machine learning (deep learning) to one-to-many problems in human-AI interactive systems. My recent publications have concentrated on NLP applications such as reply suggestion systems and text-based games. I am currently investigating the transferability of these techniques to interactive computer vision systems.
