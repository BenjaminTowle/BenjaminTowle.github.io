We introduce SeqSAM, an autoregressive approach to medical image segmentation that predicts multiple hypotheses sequentially using the Segment Anything Model. By generating diverse segmentation candidates autoregressively, SeqSAM better captures the inherent ambiguity in medical image segmentation tasks and produces more robust predictions.
